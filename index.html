About

Nischal splits his time between two sides of the classroom — working toward his BSc while also standing at the front of a room as a teacher. It's a rhythm that keeps him equally a student and a mentor, often in the same day.

Outside of coursework and lesson plans, he's drawn to open roads and unfamiliar towns, to the discipline of taekwondo, and to music that fills whatever quiet is left over. He also has a particular fondness for children — their curiosity and honesty are something he tries to carry into how he teaches.
